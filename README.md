## About Me

I'm **Atta Ullah**, a Computer Science (**Artificial Intelligence**) undergraduate at Abdul Wali Khan University Mardan. I build practical AI systems — from on-device Android classifiers and computer-vision surveillance to data-driven prediction models.

My stack spans **Python** (scikit-learn, Pandas, NumPy, OpenCV) on the AI/ML side, **Kotlin and Android** for mobile, and **TypeScript, React, and Tailwind CSS** on the web. I work with **Git and GitHub** every day.

**AdVeto** is my final-year flagship: detect ad regions on Android and classify age-aware risk on-device with a quantised multimodal model. Alongside that, I'm shipping **EasyShop** and polishing **ZYR0**.

**Turning raw data into predictions, and ideas into working projects.**
